Joystik App Signer

Signs your new app file with your upload key, right here on this device. Your key and passwords are never uploaded.

1 Your new app file

The .aab file Claude sent you (for example Joystik-1.1-unsigned.aab).

2 Your signing key

From the PWABuilder zip you downloaded in October: the file named signing.keystore.

3 Your key passwords

Open signing-key-info.txt from the same zip and copy the two passwords.

Signed and ready to upload

Save the signed app

SHA-1

Next: in Play Console, go to Test and release → Testing → Closed testing, open your track, tap Create new release and upload this file. If Play says it was signed with the wrong key, compare the SHA-1 above with Upload key certificate under Test and release → App integrity → App signing.

This page works offline once it's open, and its security settings block it from sending anything over the internet. It forgets your files and passwords when you close it.
