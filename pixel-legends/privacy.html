Privacy Policy

Pixel Legends, by James McGuire. Effective October 3, 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Pixel Legends does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

What stays on your device

Your high scores and game settings are saved on your own phone so they are there next time you play. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Ads, tracking and sharing

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell or share any data.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.
